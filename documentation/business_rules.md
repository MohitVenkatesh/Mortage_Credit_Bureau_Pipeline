# Business rules

- LTV (%) = loan amount / property value * 100; decline if greater than 95%.
- DTI (%) = monthly debt / monthly gross income * 100; decline if greater than 43%.
- FICO below 620: decline.
- FICO 620–660 and DTI above 36% (with no mandatory decline): co-signer required.
- Otherwise approve.
- Validate positive property value and income and required credit data before applying decision rules.
- Mandatory declines take precedence over co-signer.
